How to use these materials

The weekly sheets are intended to support the Thursday teaching sessions rather than to be completed entirely in class. We will normally work through selected IN CLASS problems together. Students should then attempt the YOU SHOULD TRY questions independently and use the Canvas quizzes to check understanding. EXTRA CHALLENGE questions are optional and provide additional depth.
